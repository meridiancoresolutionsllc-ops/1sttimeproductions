About the Production

Some wounds demand
to be witnessed.

"More than a play — an emotional reckoning you won't forget."

After an incredible opening weekend and a successful first run, the award-winning stage play Trauma Attraction returns to the Edge Theater for two special performances. Written and produced by 1st Time Productions, this powerful, thought-provoking theatrical experience explores the connection between love, trauma, relationships, and healing.

Through breathtaking performances, intimate storytelling, and a searing emotional landscape, the production explores how love and fear shape us, how wounds define us — and how, with courage and community, we find our way back to ourselves.

Experience the stage play that audiences are still talking about after its successful debut. Gather your family and friends and witness an unforgettable story of love, trauma, healing, and hope.

Why You Should Be There

This isn't just theatre.
It's transformation.

01

Emotional Storytelling

This production doesn't flinch. Every scene is crafted to reach you where you actually live — in love, in loss, in the unspoken and the unprocessed.

02

Live Theatrical Performance

No screen can replicate this. The energy of live theatre — the breath, the tension, the electricity between performer and audience — is irreplaceable.

03

Safe, Supportive Space

Difficult themes are explored with care and intentionality. You are welcomed, held, and guided through the experience in a community of witnesses.

04

Genuine Resilience & Healing

Walk in carrying what you carry. Walk out feeling something shift. Trauma Attraction is built to leave you changed — lighter, clearer, seen.
